# Model Spotlights

## Evo 2

- Long-context DNA modeling
- Foundation-model style scaling
- High relevance to genome-scale understanding

## JanusDNA

- Bidirectional hybrid DNA model
- Strong recent research momentum
- Useful as a representative 2025 DNA foundation model

## SPACE

- Genomic profile prediction
- Strong position in 2025 research landscape
- Focuses on predictive genomic modeling

## GENERator

- Long-context generative modeling
- Highlights sequence generation and design
- Valuable for genome generation workflows

## dnaGrinder

- Lightweight but high-capacity design
- Useful as a compact architecture example
- Helps contrast with large-scale foundation models
