# Roadmap for Emerging Genome Model Research

## Near-term goals

- Better long-context DNA modeling
- More robust evaluation benchmarks
- Better cross-species transfer

## Core research directions

- Multi-omics integration
- Long-range sequence dependency modeling
- Efficient deployment for real-world genomic analysis

## Open challenges

- Data quality and annotation coverage
- Reproducibility across species
- Biological interpretability of embeddings

## Strategic priorities

- Benchmark against clinically relevant tasks
- Combine generative and predictive modeling
- Improve domain-specific evaluation standards
