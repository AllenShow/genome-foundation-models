# 2025 Frontier Genomic Model Snapshot

## The most visible frontier models

- Evo 2 — long-context DNA and genome-scale foundation modeling
- JanusDNA — bidirectional hybrid DNA foundation model
- SPACE — genomic profile prediction and DNA foundation learning
- GENERator — generative long-context genomic modeling
- dnaGrinder — lightweight high-capacity DNA foundation model

## Why these matter

These models highlight the trend toward long-context architectures, generative modeling, and broader benchmark-driven evaluation.
