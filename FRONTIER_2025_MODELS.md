# 2025 Frontier Genomic Model Snapshot

## The most visible frontier models

- [Evo 2](https://evo2.com) — long-context DNA and genome-scale foundation modeling
- [JanusDNA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17257v4) — bidirectional hybrid DNA foundation model
- [SPACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01833v1) — genomic profile prediction and DNA foundation learning
- [GENERATOR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07272v5) — generative long-context genomic modeling
- [Gene42](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16565v1) — long-range genomic foundation model with dense attention
- [Omni-DNA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03499v1) — unified cross-modal and multi-task genomic foundation model
- [dnaGrinder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.15697v1) — lightweight high-capacity DNA foundation model

## 2026 immediate follow-up models

- [Gengram](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22203v1) — retrieval-augmented genomic foundation model
- [JEPA-DNA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17162v4) — grounding genomic FMs with joint-embedding predictive architectures

## Why these matter

These models highlight the trend toward long-context architectures, generative modeling, and broader benchmark-driven evaluation.
