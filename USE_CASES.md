# Practical Use Cases for Genomic Foundation Models

This document summarizes how DNA and genome foundation models are being applied in practice.

## Regulatory DNA analysis

- Enhancer promoter and enhancer annotation
- Rank candidate regulatory regions
- Support sequence-function mapping

## Variant interpretation

- Prioritize non-coding variants
- Flag likely pathogenic or functionally relevant mutations
- Provide sequence context for gene regulation

## Single-cell and expression modeling

- Learn gene-level context across cell states
- Model gene regulatory programs
- Support trajectory and cell-type interpretation

## Sequence generation and design

- Generate candidate regulatory DNA
- Suggest synthetic sequences with target properties
- Explore sequence-function trade-offs
